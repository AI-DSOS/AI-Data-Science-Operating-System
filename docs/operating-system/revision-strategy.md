## Overview

A 62-day bootcamp plan covers a lot of ground; without a deliberate revision strategy, earlier phases (e.g. Linear/Logistic Regression) fade by the time later phases (e.g. NLP) are underway — and that's exactly the kind of gap a real interview panel finds.

## Triggers for Revision

1. **Scheduled:** every phase transition in the bootcamp plan includes a revision pass over the previous phase's concepts, not just forward progress into the new one.
2. **Confidence-triggered:** any concept logged in the learning journal below confidence 3 gets queued, per `docs/departments/learning-mentor.md`.
3. **Interview-triggered:** any concept that causes a stumble in a Technical Interviewer mock session, especially if it repeats across 2+ sessions.

## Revision Cadence

| Trigger | When it's addressed |
|---|---|
| Phase transition | Built into the phase-transition Sunday deep-dive |
| Confidence <3 in journal | Within the same week, at the next available Study or Deep Dive block |
| Repeated interview stumble (2+ sessions) | Escalated per `docs/departments/technical-interviewer.md` — dedicated revision block, not folded into regular study |

## Revision Method

- Don't just re-read notes — redo a worked example from scratch, cold, before checking the original.
- Connect the concept to a business scenario if it wasn't already (the recurring IB-framing pattern from the regression sprint sessions).
- Update the journal entry's confidence score after revision, not just at first exposure — this is what makes the mastery rubric trend meaningful over time.

## Decision Rules

- Revision time is not "extra" — it's built into the phase-transition schedule and Sunday reserve capacity, not squeezed in only if time allows.
- A concept revised twice and still below confidence 3 is treated as a genuine gap requiring a different explanation approach, not just more repetition of the same one.

## Checklist

- [ ] Phase-transition revision pass completed before starting the new phase
- [ ] Any confidence <3 entries queued and addressed within the week
- [ ] Repeated interview stumbles escalated for dedicated revision, not just noted
- [ ] Confidence scores updated post-revision
