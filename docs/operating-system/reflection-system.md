## Overview

The scorecard tracks *what* got built. Revision Strategy tracks *concept mastery*. Reflection is different from both: it's about whether the system itself — the daily rhythm, the priority order, the time blocks — is actually working for Arulkumaran, or quietly grinding him down. A system that hits every KPI while burning someone out isn't actually working.

## What Reflection Covers (and Doesn't)

**Covers:**
- Energy and sustainability of the current schedule
- Whether the priority order (bootcamp > interview prep > projects > career > Vaagai) still feels right in practice
- Whether a department's workflow is generating real value or just busywork

**Doesn't cover** (these belong elsewhere):
- Concept-level mastery gaps → `revision-strategy.md`
- File/project counts → `docs/progress/v1-scorecard.md`
- Formal department KPI reporting → `weekly-review.md` / `monthly-board-meeting.md`

## Cadence

- **Daily:** optional, 1-line note in the journal entry if something felt notably off or notably good.
- **Weekly:** folded into the Weekly Review as an informal check — "did this week's schedule feel sustainable?"
- **Monthly:** a dedicated few minutes at the end of the Monthly Board Meeting, separate from the formal department reports.

## Prompts

Use these rather than a blank page:
- What felt like real progress this week, not just activity?
- What felt forced or rushed?
- Is Vaagai time actually staying in its Sunday-reserve lane, or creeping?
- Is the Aug 15 deadline still motivating, or starting to feel like pure pressure?

## Decision Rules

- If reflection surfaces the same concern 3 weeks running, it graduates from a journal note to a Weekly Review agenda item, and if unresolved there, to the Monthly Board Meeting.
- Reflection findings that suggest a structural problem (not just a bad week) are routed to Quarterly Review, not acted on impulsively mid-quarter.

## Checklist

- [ ] Weekly sustainability check done, even briefly
- [ ] Recurring concerns (3+ weeks) escalated rather than re-noted silently
- [ ] Monthly reflection kept separate from formal KPI reporting
