## Overview

Monthly Board Meetings check whether departments are on track against their own plans. The Quarterly Review checks whether the plans themselves are still right — a wider-lens pass that a monthly cadence is too close to the ground to catch.

## When

Every 3 months. First one: end of September 2026 (covering the DSOS build + the final stretch to the Aug 15 readiness target, plus the immediate aftermath).

## Agenda

1. **Roadmap phase pace:** how many of the 10 build phases (`AGENTS.md` Section 10) completed this quarter vs. planned.
2. **v1.0 trajectory:** at current pace, when do the five v1.0 targets (100 docs, 25 projects, 50 templates, 100+ prompts, deployed site) actually get hit — is the implicit timeline still reasonable?
3. **Career outcome check:** interview readiness trend over the full quarter, actual interviews/offers if any, and whether the target role profile (IB tech teams / FinTech startups / AI-ML consulting) still fits.
4. **Vaagai balance check:** has DSOS/job-search time been protected, or has Vaagai quietly absorbed more than the Sunday-reserve allocation intended?
5. **System check:** is the five-department structure still the right shape, or has a real gap emerged (e.g., a "Vaagai Product" department, if that venture's scope grows)?

## Inputs

- 3 months of Monthly Board Meeting minutes
- Full-quarter scorecard trend (not just the current snapshot)

## Outputs

- A written quarterly summary: what changed, what's still true, what needs to change going into next quarter
- Any structural change to the department system or roadmap sequence, documented with reasoning

## Decision Rules

- A structural change (new department, reordered phases, revised targets) only happens at Quarterly Review or later — Monthly Board Meetings can reprioritize *within* the existing structure but shouldn't redesign it, to avoid churn.
- If the Aug 15 target is missed or clearly at risk by the review before it, this is the point to decide a revised date rather than letting it quietly slide.

## Checklist

- [ ] Roadmap pace reviewed against the 10-phase plan
- [ ] v1.0 trajectory realistically projected
- [ ] Career outcomes reviewed honestly
- [ ] Vaagai time allocation checked against intent
- [ ] Structural changes (if any) documented with reasoning
