## Overview

Conferences and meetups are time-expensive relative to their per-contact value — attendance should be selective, and every event should be prepared for, not walked into cold.

## Which Events Are Worth Attending

Prioritize events with a genuine FinTech/IB technology or applied-ML focus over generic tech meetups — a room full of the right 20 people beats a room full of 200 unrelated ones. Local Chennai events get priority over travel-required ones given the time constraints in `docs/operating-system/time-blocking.md`.

## Preparation Discipline

Never attend without: a clear goal (per `templates/conference-preparation-template.md`), a refined 30-second pitch, and 1-2 specific questions prepared to ask others — preparation is what separates a productive event from a wasted evening.

## The Pitch

The 30-second pitch is the spoken version of the resume headline — same QA-to-DS positioning, refined through `prompts/career-planning/30-second-pitch-refinement.md` before each use, since the sharpest version evolves as real project results accumulate.

## Checklist

- [ ] Event genuinely relevant, not just convenient
- [ ] Goal defined before attending
- [ ] Pitch refined and rehearsed
- [ ] Follow-up plan set before the event, not improvised after
