## Overview

Networking is the highest-conversion, highest-effort-per-contact channel — it needs fewer, better contacts, not a large low-quality list.

## Target Contact Types

Priority order: (1) people currently working DS/ML roles inside investment banks or FinTech, especially anyone in a QA-to-DS or engineering-to-DS transition themselves, (2) recruiters specializing in FinTech/IB technology roles, (3) AI/ML consulting firm contacts, (4) Synechron/Morgan Stanley internal contacts who could speak to internal mobility.

## Outreach Principles

Specific over generic — reference something real (a shared connection, a specific post they wrote, a specific team). A message that could be sent to anyone gets ignored by everyone. Every outreach message should answer "why this person, why now" in the first line.

## Chennai/IB Ecosystem Context

Chennai has a meaningful IB/FinTech technology presence (Morgan Stanley, and other GCCs) — local meetups and internal cross-team connections are a genuinely underused channel relative to cold LinkedIn outreach, and should be weighted accordingly.
