## Overview

`docs/career-system/` holds the strategic reasoning behind the career transition's visibility work — target roles, positioning, and channel-specific approach. It's the layer above `templates/` (which holds the fill-in-the-blank artifacts) and complements `docs/departments/career-brand-coach.md` (which owns the operational KPIs and weekly/monthly workflow).

## How This Differs from templates/ and the Department Doc

| Layer | Answers | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `docs/departments/career-brand-coach.md` | Who does this, how often, what KPIs | "Publish ≥2 LinkedIn posts/month" |
| `docs/career-system/` (this folder) | Why this approach, what's the strategy | "Lead every piece of content with the QA-to-DS angle, because..." |
| `templates/` | What does a finished artifact look like | `templates/linkedin-post-template.md`'s fill-in structure |

## The Core Narrative

Every document in this folder anchors to the same one-sentence story, restated slightly differently per channel: **20+ years of QA automation rigor, now applied to Data Science — someone who ships production-quality work, not just notebooks.** This narrative is set once here and referenced, not reinvented per channel.
