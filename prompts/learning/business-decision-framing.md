---
title: Prompt — Business Decision Framing
purpose: Connect a pure ML concept to an investment banking decision, the recurring pattern from the regression sprint sessions.
---

## Prompt
```
If I were explaining {concept} to a Morgan Stanley risk manager who cares
about business outcomes, not math, how would I frame it? What decision does
this concept actually help someone make?
```

## Use When
Any concept that hasn't yet been tied to a business scenario.
