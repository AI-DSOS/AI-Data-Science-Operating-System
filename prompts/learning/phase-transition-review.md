---
title: Prompt — Phase Transition Review
purpose: Structured revision pass before starting a new bootcamp phase.
---

## Prompt
```
I'm finishing {phase name} and starting {next phase name}. Quiz me on the
3 most important concepts from {phase name} I'm most likely to need later,
then summarize how {next phase name} builds on them.
```

## Use When
Every bootcamp phase transition, per docs/operating-system/revision-strategy.md.
