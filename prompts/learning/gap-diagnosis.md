---
title: Prompt — Gap Diagnosis
purpose: Diagnose why a concept isn't landing, rather than just re-explaining it the same way.
---

## Prompt
```
I've been taught {concept} twice and it's still not clicking. Ask me 3
diagnostic questions to find out exactly where my understanding breaks down,
then explain just that piece differently.
```

## Use When
A concept fails revision twice per docs/operating-system/revision-strategy.md.
