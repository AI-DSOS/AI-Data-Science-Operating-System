## Prompt
```
Simulate a full interview loop for {role type}: a 15-min screen, a 45-min
technical round, and a 20-min project deep-dive. Run all three back to back
and score readiness at the end.
```

## Use When
Close to a real interview, or approaching the Aug 15 readiness checkpoint.
