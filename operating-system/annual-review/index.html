    Overview¶

    DSOS is explicitly meant to be a multi-year permanent knowledge base (per the original master prompt's success criteria), not a project that ends when the job search does. The Annual Review is where that long horizon gets checked — not just "did we hit v1.0" but "is this system still worth maintaining, and in what form."

    When¶

    Once a year — anchor date: the anniversary of the repository's founding (July 2026).

    Agenda¶

    1. Year in review: v1.0 (or beyond) scorecard trajectory across all four quarters.
    2. Career transition outcome: where did the year land — role secured, still searching, or trajectory changed — reviewed without spin.
    3. DSOS system health: are the quality gates, department structure, and documentation standard from AGENTS.md still serving the repo, or has the repo outgrown them and need a v2 governance revision?
    4. Vaagai trajectory: has the venture progressed to a point where it needs its own operating system separate from DSOS, or does it remain a Sunday-reserve-block activity?
    5. Next year's headline goal: one sentence, the way "interview readiness by Aug 15" was this year's.

    Inputs¶

    • 4 Quarterly Reviews from the year
    • Full-year scorecard history

    Outputs¶

    • A written annual summary, kept permanently in docs/operating-system/ (or journal/) as a dated record
    • Next year's headline goal and any governance revisions to AGENTS.md

    Decision Rules¶

    • Governance revisions to AGENTS.md itself (documentation standard, quality gates, department structure) are reserved for Annual Review unless something is actively broken — this protects the "module-by-module, don't churn the foundation" discipline the repo was built on.
    • If DSOS's original 5-year vision (per the master prompt) is no longer the right frame — e.g. the career transition completes and the repo's purpose shifts to ongoing professional knowledge management — that reframing happens here, explicitly, not silently.

    Checklist¶

    • [ ] Full year reviewed against the original v1.0 (or later) targets
    • [ ] Career outcome assessed honestly
    • [ ] AGENTS.md governance reviewed for continued fit
    • [ ] Vaagai's relationship to DSOS reassessed
    • [ ] Next year's headline goal set

    References¶
